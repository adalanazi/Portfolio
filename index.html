Objective

Show where water is lost and where customers complain most across five operating districts, so that action can be prioritized.

Data

Sample dataset in Excel: monthly production, billing, non-revenue water and complaints for Dammam, Khobar, Dhahran, Qatif and Jubail (60 records).

Steps

  1. Imported the Excel data and removed a duplicate “Total” row that doubled the card values.
  2. Built an NRW ratio measure in DAX: total non-revenue water divided by total produced, instead of summing percentages.
  3. Created KPI cards, district comparison charts, and district and month slicers.
  4. Checked every total against the source sheet.

Insights

  • 18.82% of produced water is not billed: 7.3M of 38.9M m³.
  • Qatif has the highest loss (27%) and the most complaints (667). Dhahran has the lowest loss (12%).
  • Dammam follows with 21% loss and 513 complaints.
  • Recommendation: prioritize loss reduction in Qatif, which leads in both loss and complaints.
